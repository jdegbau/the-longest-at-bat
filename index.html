The Longest At-Bat

On April 22, 2018, Brandon Belt of the San Francisco Giants set the record for the longest at-bat in Major League Baseball. Facing Jaime Barría of the Los Angeles Angels, Belt fouled off 16 pitches and saw 21 in all before flying out. He broke the 20-pitch record Ricky Gutierrez set against Bartolo Colón in 1998.
