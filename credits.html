The odds in this game are based on 2025 Major League Baseball averages, courtesy of Baseball Savant, FanGraphs, Baseball-Reference.com, StatMuse, and others. The full list of sources says which numbers are published and which are estimates. They are great resources for stats nerds. Seriously, check them out.

Fun facts:

  • There were 710,084 pitches thrown in 2025.
  • For any given pitch (both in the 2025 MLB season and in this game), players had a 0.27% chance of being hit by a pitch.
  • The record for the longest at-bat is 21 pitches, set by Brandon Belt against Jaime Barría on April 22, 2018. It took nearly 13 minutes.
  • The average MLB plate appearance lasts 3.9 pitches.
